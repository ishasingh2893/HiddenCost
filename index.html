All rental costs are estimates. Annualized costs are provided for comparison, not as official lease quotes.

Filters change only which apartments are shown in the detailed comparison. Saved apartments and the overall insights remain unchanged. Set a budget or calculate commutes before using those filters.

How HiddenCost calculates: Regular monthly cost = base rent + recurring fees + estimated utilities. Effective monthly cost = (regular monthly cost × lease months + one-time nonrefundable fees − rent concessions) ÷ lease months. Upfront cash is shown separately and can include refundable deposits; these deposits are not counted as an expense. Any unspecified cost may change the estimate. This tool does not verify lease terms or estimate future utility bills. Approximate Annual Leasing Cost is an annualized estimate (12 × effective monthly cost), not an official quote. Commute estimates exclude live traffic and depend on third-party OpenStreetMap geocoding and demonstration driving/walking routing services. Complete apartment street addresses are needed for route calculations; those services can be unavailable. Addresses are transmitted to them only when you click Calculate all commutes.
