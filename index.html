Advertised vs. effective monthly cost

Effective monthly cost averages nonrefundable lease costs and rent concessions across the lease term.
How HiddenCost calculates: Regular monthly cost = base rent + recurring fees + estimated utilities. Effective monthly cost = (regular monthly cost × lease months + one-time nonrefundable fees − rent concessions) ÷ lease months. Upfront cash is shown separately and can include refundable deposits; these deposits are not counted as an expense. Any unspecified cost may change the estimate. This tool does not verify lease terms or estimate future utility bills.
